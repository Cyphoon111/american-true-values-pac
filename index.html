Who We Are

American True Values PAC is a grassroots political action committee united by a common belief in the enduring principles that built this nation.

American True Values PAC was founded to give a voice to everyday Americans who believe in the timeless principles of liberty, personal responsibility, and constitutional government.

We work to support candidates and causes that reflect our shared commitment to strong families, secure communities, economic opportunity, and the freedoms guaranteed by our founding documents.

Through grassroots organizing, voter education, and direct engagement, we mobilize citizens to make their voices heard and to hold our leaders accountable to the values that make America exceptional.

Our Core Values

These principles guide everything we do and every candidate we choose to support.

🇺🇸

Faith & Family

We champion the values of faith, strong families, and the moral foundations that keep our communities thriving.

📜

Constitutional Liberty

We defend the Constitution, individual freedoms, and the God-given rights of every American citizen.

💰

Economic Freedom

We support free markets, lower taxes, and policies that create opportunity and reward hard work.

🛡️

Secure Borders

We believe in strong national security, the rule of law, and protecting the safety of American families.

🎓

Election Integrity

We stand for fair, transparent, and secure elections so that every legal vote counts.

🤝

Accountable Government

We hold elected officials accountable and demand a government that answers to the people it serves.
